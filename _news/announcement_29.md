# Keynote talk
**27 October 2026**, Dr. Hazrat Ali will be speaking as a panelist the [Scottish Ethnic Minority Talent Summit and Festival 2026](https://scottishtalentsummit.com/summit-series/fife), taking place on 27th October 2026 in Fife, Scotland. The Summit is organized by TheDataKirk, Scotland. Dr. Ali will be speaking at the panel on *AI & Data Storytelling and Community Voices - Using AI and data to amplify community voices*, alongwith Jackie Cole (The Reclaim Network), Dr Rishma Maini (NHS Fife), and Martyn Wallace (Digital Office for Scottish Local Government)
